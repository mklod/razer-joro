No BLE devices paired anymore — you already removed Joro. The issue is on the keyboard side — it still remembers the
  bonds.

  The Joro keyboard stores bonds in firmware. Unpairing from the PC only removes the PC's side of the bond. The keyboard
   still has the bond info and will only accept connections from the bonded central's MAC.

  The way to clear keyboard-side bonds is usually:
  1. Factory reset the keyboard — usually a key combination (often Fn+Esc held for 5+ seconds, or a pinhole reset)
  2. Long-press the BLE pairing button — many keyboards enter "re-pair" mode if you hold the BLE slot key for 3-5
  seconds, which clears the bond for that slot and makes it discoverable

  For Razer keyboards, the typical method is: hold the BLE slot key (Fn+1, Fn+2, or Fn+3) for 5+ seconds until the LED
  blinks fast, indicating pairing mode.

  Can you try holding one of the BLE slot keys (whichever one you want to use) for 5+ seconds to force it into pairing
  mode? The RTT logger should immediately show a successful connection if the bond is cleared.
